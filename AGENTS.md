# Repository guidance

## Documentation

- Start with [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) for decisions, sprint briefs, and
  current feature documentation.
- Read relevant numbered decisions before changing the corresponding behavior.
  Distinguish proposed policies from accepted decisions and implementation status.
- For sprint work, follow the relevant numbered brief and its acceptance criteria.
  Verify the current code; a plan is not evidence of implementation.
- Keep documentation aligned with delivered behavior. Update the sprint status,
  completion date, verification outcomes, feature guides, and index as appropriate.
- Preserve stable identifiers and historical decisions. Follow the index's naming
  conventions and link superseding decisions rather than erasing their history.
- Do not claim tests or live-provider checks passed unless they were actually run.
